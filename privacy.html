Privacy Policy

1. Information We Collect

We may collect personal information such as your name, email address, and financial goals when you voluntarily provide it to us through forms or account registration. We also collect non-personal data, including browser types and IP addresses, to analyze site traffic and improve our services.

2. Use of Information

Your information is used to provide our institutional-grade trading portfolio services, communicate with you regarding updates, and ensure compliance with regulatory standards. We do not sell or rent your personal data to third parties.

3. Web3 Wallet Connections

When you connect a Web3 wallet to utilize our copy trading algorithms, we only read public address data. We never store or transmit your private keys or recovery phrases to our servers.

4. Data Security

We implement industry-standard encryption and security protocols to protect your data. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.
